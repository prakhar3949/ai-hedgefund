# Mispriced Assets Discord - Member Guide

## Getting Access

### Step 1: Connect Whop to Discord

1. **Go to Whop.com** and log into your account
2. Click your **profile icon** (top right) → **Settings**
3. Select **Linked Accounts** from the left menu
4. Click **Connect Discord**
5. Authorize the connection when prompted
6. You should see a green checkmark next to Discord

### Step 2: Join the Discord

1. Return to your Whop dashboard
2. Click on your **Mispriced Assets** membership
3. Click the **Discord** button to join the server
4. Discord will automatically assign your member role

### Troubleshooting Access Issues

**Can't see channels?**
- Make sure your Whop account is active (not expired)
- Disconnect and reconnect Discord in Whop settings
- Leave the server, then rejoin through Whop

**Lost access suddenly?**
- Check if your subscription renewed properly on Whop
- Re-link your Discord account in Whop settings
- Contact support if payment went through but access is missing

**"You don't have permission" error?**
- Wait 5 minutes - role sync can be delayed
- Try leaving and rejoining via the Whop link

## Using MarketBot

In **#marketbot**, you can ask @MarketBot questions like:

- `@MarketBot price AMD` - Get current price
- `@MarketBot thesis COHR` - Get investment thesis
- `@MarketBot market` - Market snapshot (SPY/QQQ/IWM)
- `@MarketBot sectors` - Best/worst performing sectors
- `@MarketBot holdings` - All portfolio positions

**MarketBot only responds in #marketbot** - questions in other channels will be ignored.

## Discord Rules

### 1. No Pump & Dump / Shilling
- Don't hype random tickers without substance
- "Trust me bro" is not DD
- Share your reasoning, not just your bags

### 2. Respect the Thesis Process
- We invest based on theses, not tips
- Understand WHY we own something before asking about price action
- Read the Substack before asking basic questions

### 3. No Financial Advice
- Everything shared is for educational purposes
- We share theses, not recommendations
- You are responsible for your own decisions

### 4. Keep It Professional
- No politics outside of market-relevant policy
- No personal attacks
- Disagree with ideas, not people

### 5. Don't Spam
- One message, not five in a row
- Use threads for extended discussions
- Memes in moderation

### 6. Protect the Community
- Don't share member content publicly
- Don't screenshot alerts to post elsewhere
- What happens in Mispriced stays in Mispriced
